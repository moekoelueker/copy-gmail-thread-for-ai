# Publisher account setup

These are the account-level values to complete before uploading the first item.
They are intentionally not automated because they expose verified legal and
contact information publicly and must be confirmed by the account owner.

## Trader declaration

Assuming the extension is being published by **Zena Labs LLC** as part of its
business or professional activity, choose:

> **This is a trader account.**

The fact that the initial extension is free does not by itself make an LLC a
non-trader. If Zena Labs LLC is not actually the publishing business, stop and
confirm the legal classification with counsel before making the declaration.

## Profile values

- Publisher display name: **Zena Labs LLC**
- Contact email: **zenalabsllc@gmail.com**
- Contact email status: verified and monitored
- Address: Zena Labs LLC's current, valid business mailing address
- Phone: a current business phone number if requested
- Two-step verification: enabled for the publisher Google account

The dashboard may publicly display the trader name, address, phone number, and
email to users, especially in the European Economic Area. Review the preview
carefully before saving; do not put private account credentials or an
unreviewed personal address into a repository file.
